# Contributor agreement — draft for legal review

Not operative; do not solicit signatures until the licensing entity and terms are confirmed.

## Proposed terms

- Contributor retains copyright in their contributions.
- Contributor grants the identified project licensing entity a worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly perform/display and sublicense contributions under open-source or commercial terms.
- Contributor grants a patent license limited to claims necessarily infringed by their contribution, with scope and termination terms to be reviewed by counsel.
- Contributor confirms they have authority to grant these rights and identifies third-party material and applicable licenses; employer authorization is required where applicable.
- Contributions are supplied as-is, subject to applicable law; no obligation to accept or publish a contribution.
- The agreement must identify the legal parties, covered project and contributions, effective date, acceptance method, and treatment of prior contributions.
- Existing contributors must expressly identify and authorize prior contributions; future assent does not automatically cover earlier work.

Proposed recipient: MYLIVELAB LLC, subject to ownership and authority verification. Counsel should review the grant, patent terms, warranties, governing law, and electronic assent before adoption. A DCO alone is not a commercial relicensing grant.
