# Proposed licensing strategy — pending approval

This document is a proposal, not a license grant or a commercial offer. The existing copyright status is unchanged until the necessary permissions are obtained and a license is adopted.

## Community edition

Propose GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (AGPL-3.0-only) for the existing TideContacts community code, subject to copyright and dependency clearance. Existing contact management, personal CRM, tasks, projects, general pipelines, scripting and integrations should remain available under the community license. Commercial use under the AGPL is permitted.

## Professional edition

New professional functionality may be developed separately under commercial terms. Potential features include sales opportunity management, advanced forecasting, reporting, automation and optional synchronization. Separate repositories or process boundaries do not automatically establish license compatibility. Before distributing a combined product, obtain the necessary rights or establish a legally reviewed integration architecture.

Alternative commercial licenses for community code may be offered only to the extent that the licensing entity controls the necessary rights. MYLIVELAB LLC is the proposed commercial entity, subject to confirmation of copyright ownership and any required assignment.

Pricing, activation, support, updates and commercial end-user terms are not established by this proposal.

## Release gates

- Obtain permission from existing third-party contributors or otherwise resolve their contributions.
- Confirm rights to Ripple and audit all direct and transitive dependencies, including pinned versions.
- Confirm copyright ownership and authority of the proposed commercial entity.
- Adopt an attorney-reviewed contributor agreement with commercial relicensing rights for future contributions.
- Review the integration and distribution model and required third-party notices.
- Add the unmodified official AGPLv3 text as LICENSE, update README and package metadata, and publish the change only after clearance.
